# CLAUDE.md

Read [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md). It is the whole briefing, and it is written for any
assistant that boots in this repository or in a fork of it.

It covers what the poll says, the five constraints that will get an idea rejected
no matter how well it scores, the map of the source tree, the commands that work
on a fresh clone with no API key, and the five-step flow you are here to run with
the person who cloned this: ask them what they believe about football, turn it
into settings or code, run their version, score it against the published poll on
the identical harness, and give it a surface people can see.

Nothing project-specific lives in this file. Keeping one briefing means the
instructions cannot drift apart, and every assistant gets the same one.
