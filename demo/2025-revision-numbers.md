## Week 5, the first published poll

**25 of the 25 teams in the week-5 top 25 are in a different place once the season's answers are substituted in.** The average miss is **5.24 places** and the largest is **18**.

The largest single move inside that 25 is Navy (4-0), ranked #22 live and #40 in hindsight: over-rated live by 18 places.

Across the whole league that week the mean absolute rank change is 7.18 places over 136 ranked teams, with a maximum of 28. The league number is larger than the top-25 number and that is the finding rather than a caveat: the poll revises hardest exactly where it is least confident, which is the bottom of the table, where schedules are thin and opponents are unknown.
